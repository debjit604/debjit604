## 📖 A Little Story

I'm a cybersecurity student from **Kolkata**, currently pursuing my **B.Sc. in Cyber Security at IAER (Salt Lake)**. What started as curiosity about "how does this actually break?" turned into a daily habit of hunting bugs, running red-team engagements, and building my own offensive tooling.

Since 2024 I've been hunting vulnerabilities independently as a **bug bounty hunter**, and I've gained hands-on experience across **VAPT, red teaming, and incident response**. I also did cybersecurity internships at **CFSS** and **THIRANEX**.

I believe security knowledge should be shared, not gatekept. That's why I started **CyberSec Cohort**, a mentorship program where I guide beginners from zero to their first real findings.
